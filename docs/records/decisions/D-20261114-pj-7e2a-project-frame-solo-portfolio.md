# Project frame: solo portfolio site-time evaluator (Moon beachhead)

## Context

The repo began as a NASA Space Apps 2026 team entry (CLPS Lunar Mission Browser). The owner will build **solo**, without a team, and is not optimizing for winning the hackathon. The Space Apps brief remains useful heritage for the problem shape, not a binding schedule or eligibility target.

## Framing

Prefer a durable personal product over a 48-hour contest submission. Keep the planner-grade site×time evaluation idea (`D-20261111-pj-60ab`); drop recruitment and Sunday-deadline framing.

## Options considered

1. **A — Stay hackathon-bound**: ship for 14–15 Nov 2026 rules and team size; consequence: wrong incentive and truncated depth.
2. **B — Solo portfolio, challenge-inspired**: same core problem; free to deviate from brief, timeline, and track rules; consequence: must redefine done and claims without judging guide as north star.
3. **C — Abandon CLPS framing for a different product**: lose the accumulated domain brief and decisions.

## Decision

**B — Solo portfolio, challenge-inspired.**

## Rationale

No teammates to coordinate with; portfolio quality beats contest compliance. The challenge statement still names a real gap (rapid site/date comparison for power and DTE). Heritage data and research briefs stay; milestones and “recruit around this definition” do not.
