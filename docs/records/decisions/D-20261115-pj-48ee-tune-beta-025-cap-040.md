# Layer-1 tune: β=0.25, uplift cap 0.40; tune done, reopen if geography differs

## Context

`D-20261114-pj-6ce5` left β=0.15 and max uplift 0.30 provisional until a pre-S6 experiment, overriding the v1 defaults in `D-20261114-pj-7520`. Site01 all-2027 sweeps showed inversion rates stuck below the 2% reopen floor (`D-20261115-pj-7c70`). Follow-up metrics (variance of companion vs R; top-k overlap) on feasible windows with varying R supported choosing β in 0.15–0.40; at β=0.25 uplift never hit the old 0.30 cap on that corpus, while β≥0.35 began binding the cap and pushing top-k overlap back toward pure R.

## Framing

Lock production β / uplift for S6 green from the Site01×2027 evidence, with an explicit geographic reopen—not a second blocking study. Restate the layer-1 formula contract from `D-7520` so values stay single-sourced.

## Options considered

1. **A — Keep β=0.15 and cap=0.30 provisional** (`tune_done` false).
2. **B — β=0.25, cap=0.40, `tune_done` true**; reopen if other geography does not look like this case.
3. **C — β=0.25 and no cap**.
4. **D — β=0.25, keep cap=0.30**.

## Decision

**B.**

## Rationale

pj (2026-11-15): 0.25; it has a tangible effect and does not hit the cap. I would directly remove the cap or raise it so it simply prevents disasters (to 0.4 for example). We leave it as a decision that can be reopened when we have other geographic data, but I think it is not really necessary.

## Checks raised

- Other sites may not match Site01×2027 (many hitting the cap, too much / too little variance from B, too much / too little overlap) → accepted as the reopen trigger; ideal is that it stays like this case.
- Raising the cap to 0.40 does not change Site01×2027 at β=0.25 (0% cap hits there); it only widens the disaster rail → accepted.
